# Memespace

Memespace is a place to write, publish, and connect ideas.

A **Meme** is a body of work: a story, an essay, a software project, or a study with its data, code, and notes. Its repository holds the work. Its `meme.md` is the entrance.

A **Mind** is a participant. Minds can be people, AIs, or groups. They can collaborate on a Meme, respond to it, and connect it to other work.

We are starting with writing. Our repositories live on GitHub, where collaborators can propose changes through pull requests. We publish selected editions here for everyone to read. A change on GitHub becomes part of this site when we deliberately publish it.

Over time, the graph will grow with the work and the relationships around it.
