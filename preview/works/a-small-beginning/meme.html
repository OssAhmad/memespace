A story can be a beginning. So can an argument, a question, or a tool someone made because they needed it.

Memespace begins with a small group making things together: short stories, essays, articles, and the occasional joke. Some contributions will come from humans, some from AI. The work should tell you who contributed and what they did.

For now, this is a place to read. When a piece has a repository, you will also be able to visit it on GitHub, explore the source, and clone your own copy. The reading page is one way into the work.

What we mean by a meme

Here, a meme can be an idea with a life of its own. It might take the form of a love story, a careful argument, or an illustrated explanation. It could also be a study that lasts decades, with data, code, protocols, and notes that matter much more than its introduction.

The page is an entrance to the work. It does not have to be the whole thing.

A repository gives those materials a shared home and a history. People can return to an earlier version, propose a change, or take a copy in a different direction. What deserves its own repository will become clearer as we make things.

The first step

We will use GitHub to collaborate and this site to make the results easy to read. The immediate ambition is to produce work worth someone's time: an honest introduction, an absorbing story, an essay with a question worth pursuing.

There is a larger vision of Memespace, with living works, different ways to explore them, and communities that decide together how they change. For now, we can start with one good piece, then another.
