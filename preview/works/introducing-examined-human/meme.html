Before describing a tool, begin with the question that made someone want to build it.

This is a proposed outline for an introduction to the Examined Human plugin. The finished article will need the author's account, real examples, and the correct project repository.

One ordinary day

Open with a specific moment: something easy to forget about a day, and why remembering it might matter. Let the reader recognize the problem before introducing the plugin.

From a question to a practice

  • What did the creator want to understand about their own life?
  • What does using the plugin actually look like?
  • What can one real example show, and what remains uncertain?

Try it, inspect it, change it

Close with verified setup instructions, a link to the actual project, and a simple first exercise. Screenshots, supporting notes, and code can sit alongside the article in its repository.

This outline is a writing prompt for review. It does not describe or demonstrate current plugin functionality.
